# NMU Launches user guide

NMU Launches keeps track of everything that has to happen to open a swim program at a new site. Each launch has a list of tasks, each task has a due date and one or more people responsible, and the app reminds people by email before things are due.

## For everyone

The **Privacy** and **Terms** links at the bottom of the menu, and on the home page, explain what the app keeps and the rules for using it.

### Signing in

Go to the NMU Launches address and click **Sign in with Google**. Use the Google account your admin added. There is no password to set.

If you see "You do not have access yet", the account you used is not on the list. The page shows which email it tried. Click **Use a different account** to pick another one, or ask an admin to add you.

### My Tasks

My Tasks is your home page. It only shows tasks assigned to you, across every launch:

- **Overdue** tasks are at the top, in orange.
- **This week** is everything due in the next seven days.
- **Later** is everything after that. Click **Show more** to see the rest.
- **Done recently** shows what you finished in the last week.

Use **All**, **Due this week** and **Overdue** to filter, and **Project** to show one launch only.

The blue **Do this first** card points at the task that matters most right now, usually the one other tasks are waiting on. The **Reminders** card lists the emails coming your way.

The number next to My Tasks in the menu is how many open tasks you have.

### Updating a task

- Click the circle next to a task to mark it done. Click it again to undo.
- Click the status tag (for example "Not started") to choose **Not started**, **In progress**, **Blocked** or **Done**.
- Click the task's name to open it.

Marking a task done records you and today's date in the launch history.

A task says **Blocked** and "Waiting on …" when it needs another task finished first. It clears by itself once that task is done.

### The task page

- **Status:** the same four choices, in one row.
- **Notes:** write anything the team should know and click **Post note**. You can delete your own note for 15 minutes after posting it.
- **Links:** click **Attach a Drive or Canva link** and paste a link that starts with `https://`. The app labels it Google Drive, Canva or Other.
- **Due, Timing, Role and Assigned to:** when it is due, how that relates to Day 1, and who is responsible. Only admins can change these. **Add to Google Calendar** puts the due date on your calendar.
- **Order of work:** **Needs first** lists the tasks that must be done before this one. **Waiting on this** lists the tasks that cannot start until you finish.
- **Reminders:** which automatic emails have gone out, and any reminders you set. Click **Set a reminder**, pick a date and add a note if you like, and the app emails you that morning. If you finish the task first, the reminder is cancelled.

### The emails you get

| Email | When |
|---|---|
| **Due in 7 days** | A week before one of your tasks is due |
| **Due today** | The morning a task is due |
| **Reminder** | On a date you picked with Set a reminder |
| **Your NMU tasks for the week** | Monday mornings, if you have anything due in the next week or overdue. Overdue tasks are listed first. |
| **New task for you** | When an admin gives you a task. If you get several at once, they come in one email. |
| **You've been added to NMU Launches** | Once, when an admin adds you |

Emails are never sent for tasks that are done or skipped. Every email has a button that opens the task or My Tasks.

### Projects

**Projects** in the menu shows every launch in progress. You can open any of them to see their tasks and history, and you can update the tasks assigned to you. Only admins can change anything else.

### Partners

Partners (for example YMCA staff and swim team leads) see **Your program** instead of Projects. It only shows the launches they have been added to. They can update their own tasks, add notes and links, and set reminders. They see notes the team has shared with partners, and never internal notes or the history.

## For admins

Admins start in the **Dashboard**. The switch at the bottom of the menu lets you see the app exactly as staff do.

### The dashboard

Every launch in progress has a card with its Day 1, how much is complete, the next milestone and the lead instructor. The strip at the top counts active launches, overdue tasks, blocked tasks and the next Day 1. Sort by **Soonest Day 1** or **Most overdue**, and filter by program type or region. Click a card to open the launch.

### Starting a launch

Click **Start a launch**.

1. **Start from** a template, or from a past launch to copy its tasks, owners and timing.
2. Pick the **Site**. If the site has a profile, the partner contact, shipping address and Drive folder fill in for you. To add a site that is not on the list, type its name and choose **Add a new site**.
3. Pick **Day 1**. Every due date is worked out from it.
4. Check the program name, hours, location, lead instructors, partner contact and Drive folder. Tick **Partner access** for any partner who should see this launch.
5. On the right, **Your task list** shows every task with its due date. Open **Owners by role** to choose who fills each role. To give one task to someone else, change the person on that task.
6. **Add task** adds a task to this launch only. The template does not change.
7. Click **Create launch**. Everyone who got a task receives one email listing their new tasks.

Leave **Save the partner contact and shipping address to the site profile** ticked to reuse them next time.

### Running a launch

On a launch's page:

- **Move Day 1** asks for the new date and tells you what will happen before anything changes: "12 open tasks and their reminders will move a week later. 6 finished tasks keep their dates." After you confirm, a blue banner offers **Undo**. You can undo the latest move within 24 hours.
- **Add task** adds a task with its own name, due date, role and person. To put it between two tasks, pick what it **Needs first** and what it **Comes before**. The task after it then waits on the new one.
- Changing a due date, or adding a task in between, never moves other tasks. If a task ends up due before a task it needs first, the app warns you on the launch page, on the task, and in the dialog before you save. Change whichever date is wrong.
- Click a task's status tag to change it, or open the task to change its due date, rename it, reassign it or skip it for this launch.
- A task can have more than one person. Open it, click **Change** next to **Assigned to**, and click **Add another person**. Everyone on it sees it in My Tasks, can update it and gets its reminders. Only the people you add get a new-task email.
- **Edit details** changes the program name, hours, location, instructors, partner contact, shipping address, Drive folder and partner access.
- **History** lists every change. **See all** opens the full list.

### Closing a launch

Once Day 1 has passed, **Close launch** (in Site and partner) moves the launch to the Archive and stops its reminders. If tasks are still open, you can mark them all skipped at the same time. **Reopen** brings a closed launch back. You can still **Add task** on a closed launch, for example to record follow-up work. It gets no reminders or emails.

### The archive

**Archive** lists every closed launch with how many tasks were finished on time. Search by site name or pick a date range. Click a launch to see its numbers and each task's planned and actual dates. **Start a new launch from this** opens Start a launch with that launch's tasks.

### Templates

A template is the list of tasks for one kind of launch, with when each is due and which role does it.

- Click **Edit** to start a draft. Change a task's role from its menu, move tasks with the up and down arrows, click **Edit** on a task to change its name, timing, role or what it needs first, or **Add a task**. Click **Save draft** as you go.
- **Publish** makes the draft the new live version. Launches already running keep the tasks they were made with. Only new launches use the new version.
- **Discard** throws the draft away.
- **Version history** shows every version, who published it and what changed.
- **Duplicate** copies a template. **New template** starts a new one from a copy. **Save a project as a template** turns a launch, including tasks added to it, into a template.
- **Make inactive** hides a template from Start a launch without deleting it.
- **From past launches** appears once a template has three finished launches. It shows tasks that are often late, often skipped, or often added by hand. **Add to this template** puts an often-added task into the draft.

### Users and roles

- **Add a person** with their Google email, access level and roles. They get a welcome email and can sign in straight away.
- **Edit** changes someone's name, access level and roles, or deactivates them so they can no longer sign in. You cannot remove your own admin access, and the last admin cannot be removed.
- **Manage roles** adds, renames and deactivates roles. A role that tasks or teams use cannot be deleted, only made inactive.
- **Hand off tasks** moves all of one person's open tasks to someone else, for example when they go on leave. Anyone else on those tasks stays on them.
- **Remove personal details** appears on a deactivated person. Use it when someone asks for their information to be deleted. Their name and email become "Former team member" everywhere, and it cannot be undone. The admin runbook has the full steps. It shows the tasks first. Leave **Also put them on those launch teams** ticked so future reminders go to the new person too.
- **What each level can do** shows the permissions of Admin, Staff and Partner.
- **System** shows whether Airtable, Google sign in and email are set up. **Send me a test email** checks that email works.

Access levels:

| Level | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Admin | Everything |
| Project Lead | Same as Admin for now |
| Staff | Update their own tasks, see every launch |
| Viewer | See every launch, change nothing |
| Partner | See and update their own tasks on the launches they are added to |
