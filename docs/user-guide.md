# NMU Programs user guide

NMU Programs keeps track of everything that has to happen to open a swim program at a new site. Each program has a list of tasks, each task has a due date and one or more people responsible, and the app reminds people by email before things are due.

## For everyone

The **Privacy** and **Terms** links at the bottom of the menu, and on the home page, explain what the app keeps and the rules for using it.

### Signing in

Go to the NMU Programs address and click **Sign in with Google**. Use the Google account your admin added. There is no password to set.

To sign out, click **Sign out** at the bottom of the menu. If you used a shared computer or lost a laptop or phone, click **Sign out of all devices** instead (from any device where you are still signed in): every session you have ends, and you sign in again with Google.

If you see "You do not have access yet", the account you used is not on the list. The page shows which email it tried. Click **Use a different account** to pick another one, or ask an admin to add you.

### My Tasks

My Tasks is your home page. It only shows tasks assigned to you, across every program:

- **Overdue** tasks are at the top, in orange.
- **This week** is everything due in the next seven days.
- **Later** is everything after that. Click **Show more** to see the rest.
- **Done recently** shows what you finished in the last week.

Use **All**, **Due this week** and **Overdue** to filter, and **Project** to show one program only. **State**, **City** and **Program type** narrow it further, and you can combine them. State and City only appear once an admin has filled in those fields for your sites. **Sort by** orders each group by **Due date** (the default), **Program**, or **Location** (state, then city; tasks with no location go last).

Your choices are kept in the page address, so refreshing keeps them, and you can bookmark a view such as "my overdue Learn to Swim tasks in WA". A shared link only ever shows the tasks of whoever opens it.

The blue **Do this first** card points at the task that matters most right now, usually the one other tasks are waiting on. The **Reminders** card lists the emails coming your way.

The number next to My Tasks in the menu is how many open tasks you have.

### Updating a task

- Click the circle next to a task to mark it done. Click it again to undo.
- Click the status tag (for example "Not started") to choose **Not started**, **In progress**, **Blocked** or **Done**.
- Click the task's name to open it.

Marking a task done records you and today's date in the program history.

A task says **Blocked** and "Waiting on …" when it needs another task finished first. It clears by itself once that task is done.

### The task page

- **Status:** the same four choices, in one row.
- **Notes:** write anything the team should know and click **Post note**. You can delete your own note for 15 minutes after posting it.
- **Links:** click **Attach a Drive or Canva link** and paste a link that starts with `https://`. The app labels it Google Drive, Canva or Other.
- **Files and photos:** click **Upload a file or photo** and choose one from your computer. Photos, PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text and CSV files work, up to 5 MB each. For anything bigger, put it in Google Drive and attach the link. Anyone who can see the task can open the file.
- **Due, Timing, Role and Assigned to:** when it is due, how that relates to Day 1, and who is responsible. Only admins can change these. **Add to Google Calendar** puts the due date on your calendar.
- **Order of work:** **Needs first** lists the tasks that must be done before this one. **Waiting on this** lists the tasks that cannot start until you finish.
- **Reminders:** which automatic emails have gone out, and any reminders you set. Click **Set a reminder**, pick a date and add a note if you like, and the app emails you that morning. If you finish the task first, the reminder is cancelled.

### The emails you get

| Email | When |
|---|---|
| **Due in 7 days** | A week before one of your tasks is due |
| **Due today** | The morning a task is due |
| **Reminder** | On a date you picked with Set a reminder |
| **Your NMU tasks for the week** | Monday mornings, if you have anything due in the next week or overdue. Overdue tasks are listed first. |
| **New task for you** | When an admin gives you a task. If you get several at once, they come in one email. |
| **Reminder from [admin]** | When an admin clicks **Nudge** on one of your tasks, sometimes with a short message from them |
| **You've been added to NMU Programs** | Once, when an admin adds you |

Emails are never sent for tasks that are done or skipped. Every email has a button that opens the task or My Tasks.

### Putting your tasks on Google Calendar

There are two ways. You can use either or both.

- **One task at a time.** On a task's page, click **Add to Google Calendar** and save the event. It is a one-off copy: if the task's date or name changes later, change the event yourself.
- **All your tasks, kept up to date.** On **My Tasks**, in the **Calendar** box, tick **Sync all my tasks to Google Calendar**, then click **Add to Google Calendar** and confirm. Your open tasks with a due date appear as all-day events titled "[Program] Task". When a task's date or name changes, or it is done, skipped or given to someone else, your calendar follows on its own. Google checks for changes a few times a day, so new tasks and changes can take several hours to appear.

With sync on, a task's page says **Already on your calendar via sync**, so you do not add it twice.

**If the Add to Google Calendar button does not work,** copy the link with **Copy**. In Google Calendar on a computer, next to **Other calendars** click **+**, then **From URL**, paste the link and click **Add calendar**. The same link works in Apple Calendar and Outlook.

**Keep the link private.** Anyone with it can see your task list. If you shared it by mistake, click **Reset link**: the old link stops working at once, and you add the new one to Google Calendar again (and remove the old "NMU Programs tasks" calendar there).

**To stop,** click **Turn off**. The link stops working, and Google removes the events the next time it checks. You can also remove the calendar in Google Calendar under **Other calendars**.

### Projects

**Projects** in the menu shows every program in progress. You can open any of them to see their tasks and history, and you can update the tasks assigned to you. Only admins can change anything else.

### Partners

Partners (for example YMCA staff and swim team leads) see **Your program** instead of Projects. It only shows the programs they have been added to. They can update their own tasks, add notes, links and files, and set reminders. They see notes the team has shared with partners, and never internal notes or the history.

## For admins

Admins start in the **Dashboard**. The switch at the bottom of the menu lets you see the app exactly as staff do.

### The dashboard

Every program in progress has a card with its Day 1, how much is complete, the next milestone and the lead instructor. The strip at the top counts active programs, overdue tasks, blocked tasks and the next Day 1. Sort by **Soonest Day 1** or **Most overdue**, and filter by program type or region. Click a card to open the program.

### Starting a program

Click **Start a program**.

1. **Start from** a template, or from a past program to copy its tasks, owners and timing.
2. Pick the **Site**. If the site has a profile, the partner contact, shipping address and Drive folder fill in for you. To add a site that is not on the list, type its name and choose **Add a new site**. To add a template, choose **+ Add a template** under the templates. It starts as a copy of one you have, and you can change its tasks later on Templates.
3. Pick **Day 1**. Every due date is worked out from it.
4. Check the program name, hours, location, lead instructors, partner contact and Drive folder. Tick **Partner access** for any partner who should see this program.
5. On the right, **Your task list** shows every task with its due date. Open **Owners by role** to choose who fills each role. To give one task to someone else, change the person on that task.
6. **Add task** adds a task to this program only. The template does not change.
7. Click **Create program**. Everyone who got a task receives one email listing their new tasks.

Leave **Save the partner contact and shipping address to the site profile** ticked to reuse them next time.

### Running a program

On a program's page:

- **Overdue** at the top lists every open task that is past its due date, most overdue first. While a task is overdue it shows only there, not in its phase. Once it is done or skipped, or its date moves, it goes back to its phase. The section is hidden when nothing is overdue.
- **Move Day 1** asks for the new date and tells you what will happen before anything changes: "12 open tasks and their reminders will move a week later. 6 finished tasks keep their dates." After you confirm, a blue banner offers **Undo**. You can undo the latest move within 24 hours.
- **Add task** adds a task with its own name, due date, role and person. To put it between two tasks, pick what it **Needs first** and what it **Comes before**. The task after it then waits on the new one.
- Changing a due date, or adding a task in between, never moves other tasks. If a task ends up due before a task it needs first, the app warns you on the program page, on the task, and in the dialog before you save. Change whichever date is wrong.
- Click a task's status tag to change it, or open the task to change its due date, rename it, reassign it or skip it for this program.
- **Nudge** (at the top of a task's page) emails everyone on the task a reminder right away, on top of the automatic ones. It shows for open tasks that have at least one active person on them. Add a short message if you like (up to 500 characters), then click **Send reminder**. Each person can be nudged about the same task once a day, and gets at most 3 nudges a day in total from all admins, and each admin can send up to 20 nudges an hour. The task then shows **Last nudged by [name], [date]**, and the history says who nudged whom. Your message goes only in the email; it is not saved. To reach a person's task from **Assignments**, click their name, then the task.
- **Delete task** (in a task's status menu, or on the task's page) removes a task for good. The dialog lists any tasks waiting on it; they stop waiting. The history keeps a line saying what was deleted, its due date, who it was assigned to and who deleted it. Its notes stay in the history, and its links and files move to the program's Links and files. It cannot be undone, so if you might need the task later, **skip** it instead. Closed programs cannot have tasks deleted. If you see "The task was not fully deleted", Airtable had a problem part way through: click **Delete task** again to finish.
- A task can have more than one person. Open it, click **Change** next to **Assigned to**, and click **Add another person**. Everyone on it sees it in My Tasks, can update it and gets its reminders. Only the people you add get a new-task email.
- **Edit details** changes the program name, hours, location, instructors, partner contact, shipping address, Drive folder and partner access.
- **History** lists every change. **See all** opens the full list.

### Closing a program

Once Day 1 has passed, **Close program** (in Site and partner) moves the program to the Archive and stops its reminders. If tasks are still open, you can mark them all skipped at the same time. **Reopen** brings a closed program back. You can still **Add task** on a closed program, for example to record follow-up work. It gets no reminders or emails.

### Exporting a program to a spreadsheet

On a program's page, click **Export** at the top. Your browser downloads a CSV file named after the program and today's date, for example `Bellevue-Swim-Safely-tasks-2026-11-12.csv`. Only admins see the button. It works on running and closed programs.

The file has one row per task, soonest due date first, with these columns: Program, Task, Assigned to, Status, Due date, Completed date, Phase, Role, Program type and Site type. Tasks with nobody on them say **Unassigned**. Tasks without a due date are at the bottom. A program with no tasks gives a file with just the column names.

To open it in Google Sheets, either:

- **Import it into a sheet.** Open Google Sheets, start a blank spreadsheet, then **File > Import > Upload** and pick the file. Choose **Replace current sheet** and leave the separator on **Detect automatically**.
- **Or upload it to Drive.** In Google Drive, **New > File upload** and pick the file, then right-click it and choose **Open with > Google Sheets**.

Dates come through as real dates, so you can sort and filter by them. The export is a copy: changes made in the spreadsheet do not go back into NMU Programs. Do not share it outside NMU, since it lists who is doing each task.

A task name that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is saved with an apostrophe in front, for example `'=Order caps`, so the spreadsheet shows it as text instead of running it as a formula. Some spreadsheet apps show the apostrophe; that is expected.

### The archive

**Archive** lists every closed program with how many tasks were finished on time. Search by site name or pick a date range. Click a program to see its numbers and each task's planned and actual dates. **Start a new program from this** opens Start a program with that program's tasks.

### The audit trail

**Audit** lists every change made with admin access, newest first: when, which admin, what they did (for example "Changed a person" or "Deleted a task") and which person, program or task it was about. Pick an admin or a period to narrow it. Nobody can edit or delete these entries, not even admins, so it is the place to check who changed someone's access or deleted something. Hover over an entry to see its technical name and request number, which help when reporting a problem.

### Templates

A template is the list of tasks for one kind of program, with when each is due and which role does it.

- Click **Edit** to start a draft. Change a task's role from its menu, move tasks with the up and down arrows, click **Edit** on a task to change its name, timing, role or what it needs first, or **Add a task**. Click **Save draft** as you go.
- **Publish** makes the draft live. Programs already running keep the tasks they were made with. Only new programs use the changes.
- **Discard** throws the draft away.
- **Version history** shows every version, who published it and what changed.
- **Duplicate** copies a template. **New template** starts a new one from a copy. **Save a project as a template** turns a program, including tasks added to it, into a template.
- **Make inactive** hides a template from Start a program without deleting it.
- **Delete** removes a template, with its tasks and version history, for good. It only works if no program, running or archived, was ever made from it. Otherwise, make it inactive instead.
- **From past programs** appears once a template has three finished programs. It shows tasks that are often late, often skipped, or often added by hand. **Add to this template** puts an often-added task into the draft.

### Assignments

Lists every active person, busiest first, with how many open tasks they have. Click a name to see the active programs they are on and their open tasks on each.

### Users and roles

- Click a person's name (or **Edit**) to change their name, access level and roles.
- **Add a person** with their Google email, access level and roles. They get a welcome email and can sign in straight away.
- **Edit** changes someone's name, access level and roles, or deactivates them so they can no longer sign in. You cannot remove your own admin access, and the last admin cannot be removed. Changing someone's access level, taking a role away or deactivating them also signs them out everywhere, so they sign in again with their new access.
- **Sign out everywhere** (in **Edit**) ends every session that person has, on every device. Use it if they lose a laptop or phone or think someone else used their account. They can sign in again straight away.
- **Manage roles** adds, renames and deactivates roles. A role that tasks or teams use cannot be deleted, only made inactive.
- **Hand off tasks** moves all of one person's open tasks to someone else, for example when they go on leave. Anyone else on those tasks stays on them.
- **Remove personal details** appears on a deactivated person. Use it when someone asks for their information to be deleted. Their name and email become "Former team member" everywhere, and it cannot be undone. The admin runbook has the full steps. It shows the tasks first. Leave **Also put them on those program teams** ticked so future reminders go to the new person too.
- In developer mode only, **What each level can do** shows the permissions of Admin, Staff and Partner, and **System** shows whether Airtable, Google sign in and email are set up. **Send me a test email** checks that email works. Neither shows in production.

Access levels:

| Level | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Admin | Everything |
| Staff | Update their own tasks, see every program |
| Viewer | See every program, change nothing |
| Partner | See and update their own tasks on the programs they are added to |
